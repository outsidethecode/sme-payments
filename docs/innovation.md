Payments are slow because verification is manual and trust is external.
If payment conditions are digitally verifiable, money can move immediately — or be financed cheaply while locked.

Your winning primitives are:
- Conditional payment locking
- Event-driven release (delivery, acceptance, milestone)
- Receivables that are pre-verified and therefore financeable
- Liquidity attached to the transaction, not the balance sheet

That is exactly what “future payment technologies” means here.
